|               | Disease                                | Health                                     |                                                       |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Positive Test | TP                                     | FP                                         | Positive Predictive Value<br>$$PPV=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}$$ |
| Negative Test | FN                                     | TN                                         | Negative Predictive Value<br>$$NPV=\frac{TN}{TN+FN}$$ |
|               | Sensitivity<br>$$TP=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}$$ | Specificity<br>$$TN=\frac{TN}{TN+FP}$$<br> |                                                       |


Sensitivity and specificity refers to the ability of the test of find a diseased person or not. A high specificity guarantees a high probability of find a person with the disease, a high sensitivity guarantees a high probability of classified as healthy a healthy person.

PPV and NPV refer to the probability of a person tested and resulted positive or negative being truly positive or truly negative.
