"..complex phenotype with a fundamental role in many aspects of cognition and behavior. Broadly characterized as a predisposition toward rapid, unplanned responses to stimuli, impulsivity has been implicated in multiple psychiatric and neurological conditions, maladaptive decision making and risk-taking behaviors."

Eight dimensions of impulsivity:
- Attention
- Motor
- Negative urgency
- Nonplanning
- Lack of perseverance
- Positive urgency: tendency to act rashly while in an intense emotional state. A recent paper (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9292904/) suggests no difference in respect to positive and negative emotions.
- Lack of premeditation
- Sensation seeking

Identified by Barratt Impulsiveness Scale and UPPS-P
