Simultanagnosia is characterized by the inability to perceive more than one object at a time, and is categorized into dorsal and ventral subtypes. Dorsal simultanagnosia (DS) is characterized by difficulty in perceiving the spatial arrangement of multiple objects: patients with DS can recognize individual objects, but have difficulty perceiving how the objects are related. In contrast, patients with VS can perceive the spatial arrangement of objects but have difficulty recognizing and identifying them, resulting in slow and piecemeal processing of complex scenes as well as letter-by-letter reading.
